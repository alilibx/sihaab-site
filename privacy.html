Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Who we are

Sihaab ("we", "us") operates sihaab.com from Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at hello@sihaab.com.

2. What we collect

This website is a marketing site. The only personal data we receive is what you choose to send us:

  • Your email address and message, when you use the waitlist form or email us. The form opens your own email app; nothing is submitted to our servers in the background.

We do not run user accounts or payment processing through this website. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

3. What we do not collect

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking scripts.
  • Your language and theme preferences are stored only in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device.

One technical exception: this site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them directly from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details in that request. We plan to self-host the fonts to remove this.

4. Why we process your data

We use your email address to respond to you and to contact you about Sihaab, including waitlist updates. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by contacting us. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Emails you send us are processed by our email hosting provider as a service provider on our behalf. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

6. Retention

We keep waitlist emails until you ask us to remove you, or until they are no longer needed for launch communication, whichever comes first.

7. Your rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent. Email hello@sihaab.com and we will act on your request.

8. International transfers

Emails may be stored or processed in the countries where our email provider operates, and font requests are served by Google as described above. We choose providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

9. Children

This site is not directed at children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The date at the top reflects the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@sihaab.com.
